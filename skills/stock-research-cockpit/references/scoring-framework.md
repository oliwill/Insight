# Research Score Framework

Research Score measures company/thesis quality, not whether now is a good entry.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | What to inspect |
|---|---:|---|
| Industry / TAM | 20% | Sector growth, market size, structural tailwinds, capital flow |
| Moat | 20% | IP, switching costs, scale, network effects, customer lock-in |
| Growth quality | 20% | Revenue growth, margin trend, FCF, recurring revenue, durability |
| Valuation | 25% | P/S, PSG, forward PE, DCF, relative valuation, margin of safety |
| Team / governance | 15% | CEO quality, insider ownership/trading, SBC, dilution, board quality |

## Score Bands

| Total | Label | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 75-100 | High conviction candidate | Strong thesis quality, still requires timing discipline |
| 60-74 | Standard candidate | Worth researching or owning if timing and risks are acceptable |
| 45-59 | Watch | Needs more evidence, better valuation, or clearer catalyst |
| 0-44 | Pass | Thesis quality is too weak or evidence is insufficient |

## Evidence Adjustment

Increase confidence when claims are supported by primary sources, filings, transcripts, verified data, or multiple independent sources.

Reduce confidence when:

- Important inputs are missing.
- Evidence is mostly social sentiment or unsourced commentary.
- Valuation requires heroic assumptions.
- The core thesis depends on one unverified catalyst.

## Output

Always show:

- Total score out of 100.
- Dimension scores.
- Confidence level.
- Top positive driver.
- Top weakness.
- Missing evidence that could change the score.
